The Experience

Your weekend long experience at Fyre Festival is more than music, for the adventurous, join our half-day Exuma excursions. For those who’d like to amplify their experience, enjoy a cabana at the Fyre Beach Club or a VIP table overlooking the main stage. Itinerary additions (purchased in addition to your ticket) will be available for purchase in the weeks leading up to the festival.

Kickoff BBQ
Each Thursday evening at Fyre Festival you can join our FyreStarters for a beachside kick-off BBQ. Enjoy a stunning sunset, a Bahamian menu and champagne toast to celebrate the weekend to come.
Cabana @ Fyre Beach Club
Lock down a cabana for a full or half-day and watch the waves go by, while enjoying drinks oceanside at the Fyre Beach Club.
VIP Main Stage Tables
Watch Fyre Festival main stage performances in style with a VIP table. You’ll be in very good company.
Yacht Brunch Party
Board a catamaran for a late morning cruise of the Exumas. Enjoy a champagne brunch and awesome views.
Explore the Exumas by Private Aircraft
Start your day just after breakfast with a scenic flight to Staniel Cay. After landing, take a quick boat ride to Thunderball Cave for some snorkeling and cave diving in the spirit of James Bond. Then, drop by Pig Beach for an opportunity to swim with the pigs. After a quick lunch at Staniel Cay Yacht Club, we’ll fly over to Dean’s Blue Hole, and then make our way back just in time to catch the music.
Check back soon on how to add these experiences (purchased at an additional cost) to your Fyre Festival itinerary.

Getting Here

All packages include complimentary flights to and from the Exumas. Flights will depart to the Bahamas from Miami on Thursday and Friday, and return to Miami from the Bahamas on Sunday and Monday. You may choose any combination of flights. For example, you could leave for the Bahamas Friday morning and return to Miami on Monday morning, or leave Friday evening and return Sunday evening. We will do our best to accommodate your preferences. Flight departure times will be scheduled on a first come, first serve basis after you have completed your traveler preference form. Your traveler preference form will be sent to you within a few days after you purchase your ticket. If you have already purchased a ticket package, please make sure everyone in your travel party fills out a traveler profile form. This will expedite your flight assignment. For more information on travel, please visit our FAQ.
