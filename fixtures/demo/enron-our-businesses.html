our businesses
Enron is changing the way the world thinks about energy and communications.

We are an energy network that has championed restructuring natural gas and electricity markets globally. Our goal is to help customers make the most of the enormous opportunities that competitive energy markets offer.

  • We help make the competitive U.S. energy markets more efficient. We market and deliver physical commodities and design financial risk management tools to protect customers from price and volume volatility.

  • Our outsourcing services enable commercial and light industrial companies, schools, universities and other institutions to reduce capital costs and limit their energy risk at one or multiple sites.

  • We are the world's largest marketer of electricity and natural gas. We participate in markets in North America, South America, Northern Europe, the United Kingdom, Continental Europe and Australia.

  • In Asia, Africa and India, we're developing essential energy infrastructure and offering financial services to customers. We're introducing comprehensive energy systems in South America, the Caribbean and the Middle East.

  • Enron is applying its special skills to the Internet, too. Enron Communications' Intelligent Network technology enables businesses to speed data, voice and video over the web. We also see opportunities in bandwidth trading, where standardized products will enable companies to efficiently buy and sell bandwidth capacity.

  • Enron has capital and assets -- approximately $34 billion worth. We operate some of the largest natural gas pipeline systems in North America and South America and we are introducing more flexible storage capacity. We're offering our natural gas customers new options to manage their gas portfolios better.

  • Enron has developed, owns or operates more than 14,350 megawatts of electricity worldwide. Portland General Electric, an Enron subsidiary, is the largest electric company in the state of Oregon and a leading proponent of competitive energy markets.

  • We offer a full range of financial services. When appropriate, we invest in our customers to strengthen their capitalization and allow them to pursue new business opportunities.
For four years running, Fortune magazine has named Enron the "Most Innovative Company in America" and last year named us one of the "100 Best Companies to Work for in America." We will continue to use our people, our knowledge and our experience to discover new ways to make energy, communications and infrastructure development better.
